Chuckles in the City
"Chuckles in the City" is a video game originally released on March 1st, 2022, on Scratch.
It has since been updated several times, adding new content and fixes.
The game involves you controlling the character "Chuckles," as you travel into the city to use a mech to defeat "The Floating Head."

Chuckles
Chuckles is the main protagonist of the video game "Chuckles in the City."
He is a yellow stickman and typically has red eyes, though he is sometimes depicted as having orange eyes.

The Floating Head
The Floating Head is one of Chuckles' nemeses.
It is mysterious in that it has no known origins or motives other than to cause mass destruction.
